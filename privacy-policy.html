Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026  |  Effective: March 2025

✅ Our Core Privacy Promise

Digital Will Locker is built to keep your vault on your device. Your vault is stored on your device and encrypted with AES-256, and we do not receive it in normal use. A few features use the internet, and Sections 3 and 10 explain exactly what they send.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be completely transparent. The following data is never collected by us:

  • Your vault contents, documents, wills, or files
  • Your password in readable form, or your biometric data
  • Your 12-word recovery phrase
  • Your payment card details stored in the app
  • Your browsing or app usage behavior
  • The contents of your vault, unless you use an emergency feature described in Section 3

2. How Your Data Is Stored

The data you enter into Digital Will Locker is stored on your device:

  • AES-256 military-grade encryption for your vault data (assets, cards, contacts, notes and document details) and for the document files you add — AES is the encryption algorithm standard approved by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
  • The encryption key is created and kept in your device's secure hardware
  • PBKDF2 password hashing with 10,000 iterations
  • Biometric data handled by your device secure enclave only
  • Your vault is not stored on our servers, and we make no backups of it
  • No internet connection is needed to open and use your vault
  • Document Organizer, which categorizes and tags your uploaded documents, runs entirely on your device and works offline — it does not send your document content anywhere

3. Features That Use The Internet

The following features send information over HTTPS to our service providers. Nothing is sent unless you use the feature.

  • Account sign-in and subscription: your account sign-in details (such as your email address) and your subscription status are stored with our account service provider, Google Firebase. We cannot read your password. Signing in again after a reinstall restores your account and subscription, but not your vault documents, which stay only on the device where you saved them.
  • Emergency alerts (Dead Man's Switch): when the switch triggers, the app sends an email to your trusted contacts through our email provider. The email contains your name, your phone number and a link. Your trusted contacts' names and email addresses are used to send it.
  • Emergency document sharing: when the switch triggers, your documents are uploaded to temporary online storage (Supabase) so that your trusted contact can download them. Files are deleted once the contact has downloaded them or chooses to delete them.
  • Emergency Document QR: this uploads a copy of one document to the same storage and puts a link to it in a QR code. Anyone who has the QR code can open that document, so share it only with someone you trust.
  • AI features: your chat text or voice audio is sent through our server to an AI service provider (see Section 5).
  • Payments: handled by PayPal (see Section 9).
  • Update check: the website version of the app may ask our website whether a newer version is available.

4. App Permissions We Request

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Biometrics: For fingerprint/face ID login — handled by your device only
  • Storage: To save and access encrypted files locally on your device
  • Microphone: For the optional AI Voice Chat, recording Legacy Messages, and the Noise Detector
  • Notifications: For Dead Man's Switch check-in reminders and Legacy Message reminders
  • Camera: Optional — for scanning documents, scanning QR codes and recording video messages
  • Location: Optional — used only by emergency features

5. AI Features & Third-Party Services

The app includes 9 built-in AI tools: Emergency AI, Home Remedies, Noise Detector, Mood Listener, Cost of Living, Wellness Guide, Life Skills, AI Travel Guide, and AI Image Generator.

The AI chat gives built-in guidance offline for questions about the app itself (how features work, what a plan includes, how to use the vault, and similar). An internet connection is needed when you ask it for additional information beyond the app, such as general knowledge, when you use voice questions, and when you use the AI Image Generator or AI Travel Guide.

Where an AI feature does use a third-party AI service provider (for example, voice transcription in AI Voice Chat, or an AI chat request that needs information beyond the app):

  • Only the specific voice audio or chat query is sent, through our server, to our AI service provider (opt-in only, and only when internet is used for that request)
  • That data is processed by our provider under their own privacy policy
  • The app does not send your vault data to any AI service provider. Anything you type or say in an AI request is sent, so avoid including sensitive details
  • You can use the entire app, including your vault and Document Organizer, without ever using an internet-dependent AI feature

6. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, the following applies under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Data Controller: Digital Will Locker acts as the data controller for the data described in Section 3. We are not a data controller for your vault contents, which stay on your device unless you use an emergency feature
  • Lawful Basis for Processing: Where processing occurs (for example account sign-in, emergency features or optional AI features), it is based on your consent or on what is needed to provide the feature you asked for. You may withdraw consent at any time by not using that feature
  • Your GDPR Rights: Right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port any data we hold about you; right to object to processing; right to withdraw consent at any time; right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority
  • International Transfers: Your account details, emergency alerts and emergency documents are processed by our service providers (Google Firebase, Supabase and our email provider), and AI requests by our AI provider. Their servers may be outside your country, under their own safeguards. Your vault stays on your device unless you use an emergency feature
  • Data Retention: We do not retain your vault. Emergency documents stay in temporary storage until they are downloaded or deleted. Account details are kept while your account exists. Data processed via AI features (e.g. voice audio) is handled per our provider's retention policy

7. Asia-Pacific Privacy Law Compliance

Digital Will Locker also complies with the following Asia-Pacific data protection frameworks. Because your vault stays on your device and we receive only the limited data described in Section 3, compliance under each of these laws follows the same core principle: we process as little personal data as possible, and only for the feature you use.

  • Malaysia — Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010, as amended 2024: We do not receive your vault in normal use. Where data is processed (account sign-in, emergency features or optional AI features), it is done with your consent, and you may request access, correction, or withdrawal of consent at any time by contacting us below
  • Singapore — Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA): We follow the same consent, purpose limitation, and data minimisation principles for any Singapore-based users. No personal data is collected, used, or disclosed without your consent, and your vault contents are not transmitted to us except through emergency features you turn on
  • India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023: As a Digital Personal Data Fiduciary where applicable, we process only the limited data described in Section 3 (Features That Use The Internet), based on your consent. You retain the right to access, correct, and erase any such data, and to withdraw consent at any time
  • China — Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL): We do not target users in mainland China. If you use the account or emergency features, the data described in Section 3 may be processed. Any processing is limited to what is necessary and based on your consent
  • Japan — Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI): We handle any voluntarily provided data (e.g. for optional AI features) in line with APPI's purpose-specification and consent requirements, and do not share data with third parties beyond what is disclosed in Sections 3 and 5
  • South Korea — Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA): The same minimal-data principle applies — your vault is not collected, and data used by internet features is processed only with informed consent

If you are located in any of these jurisdictions and have questions about your specific rights under local law, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

  • Right to know what personal information we collect (see Section 3; we do not collect your vault in normal use)

9. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are processed securely through PayPal. This app does not use Google Play billing for subscriptions. We never see or store your full payment card details. All billing is handled by PayPal under their own privacy policy at paypal.com/privacy.

10. Dead Man's Switch

If you enable this feature, the app monitors your check-in activity based on the inactivity period you choose. Checking in and the local countdown work offline. If you don't check in before that period ends, the app needs an internet connection to send the emergency email to your trusted contacts and to upload your documents to temporary online storage. The email contains your name, your phone number and a link. To open the documents, your trusted contact must call you twice and wait 24 hours in the app, which gives you a final chance to cancel if you are simply unreachable. Once the documents have been downloaded, they are deleted from our storage. Outside of this emergency workflow, your vault is not transmitted anywhere.

Because your vault stays on your device, it cannot be affected by a breach on our end, except for documents held temporarily in storage through emergency features.
